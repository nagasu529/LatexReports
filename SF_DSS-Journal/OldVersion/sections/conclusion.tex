\section{Conclusion}

This study presents a segment-aware explainable AI framework that integrates clustering, SHAP-based interpretation, and surrogate rule extraction to support targeted agricultural skill development. By combining farmer segmentation with interpretable model explanations, the framework moves beyond prediction to provide transparent, data-driven decision rules that can be operationalized by extension practitioners. The findings highlight the importance of accounting for heterogeneous learning pathways, as low-, moderate-, and high-skill farmers exhibit distinct drivers of training effectiveness.

The proposed Segment–Attribute–Recommend (SAR) architecture is generalizable to other agricultural training contexts and aligns with ongoing efforts to promote interpretable and accountable AI in agricultural decision support systems. In the context of Thailand’s Smart Farmer program, the framework offers a practical mechanism for improving the precision and equity of training allocation. Future work should validate the system in prospective field trials and explore its integration into digital extension platforms to enhance its scalability and policy relevance.
