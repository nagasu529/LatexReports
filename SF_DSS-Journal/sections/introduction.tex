\section{Introduction}

Agricultural skill development plays a critical role in enabling smallholder farmers to adopt modern
production practices, improve resource efficiency, and enhance productivity outcomes. Prior research
has shown that heterogeneity in farmer capabilities strongly influences technology adoption, learning
trajectories, and the effectiveness of extension programs \citep{feder1985adoption, davis2012impact}. 
In Thailand, the Smart Farmer program aims to strengthen farmer competencies across marketing, 
production management, and value-added processing.

Recent advances in machine learning (ML) and explainable artificial intelligence (XAI) offer new
opportunities to support agricultural decision-making by uncovering complex behavioral patterns and
generating transparent, actionable insights \citep{adadi2018xai, doran2017xai}. While ML has been widely applied
in precision agriculture, crop modeling, and environmental monitoring \citep{hengl2018rf, ferreira2020mlagri}, 
its use in human-centered agricultural extension—particularly in skill development—remains limited.

This study proposes a segment-aware explainable AI framework integrating clustering, SHAP-based
interpretation, and surrogate rule extraction to support targeted agricultural skill development.
